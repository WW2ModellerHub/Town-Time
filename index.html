ABOUT THIS ARCHIVE

Photographs first. Stories clearly labelled.

Dated photographs are individually credited images with a stated capture date and a link to the original Wikimedia Commons file page. Town:Time does not generate or fabricate historical photographs.

Historical stories are text records about a place or event. They are intentionally shown without a pretend period image, and approximate dates are labelled as such.

The map is a present-day reference map supplied by OpenFreeMap from OpenStreetMap data. The timeline only filters archive records. If the map service or map library fails, the app replaces it with a readable fallback instead of leaving patchy tiles.

Comparisons use two independently dated archive photographs. Camera positions may differ, so the slider is illustrative rather than a pixel-aligned reconstruction.
