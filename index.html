THE PROJECT / TRANSPARENCY

A time machine built from real evidence.

Town:Time pairs present-day maps with dated, geolocated historical photographs. Hinckley includes an editorially curated starting archive. Other UK towns can discover geolocated Wikimedia Commons images, but those discoveries are not assigned historical dates without verification.

The map is present-day OpenStreetMap: moving the timeline filters records but does not reconstruct historic streets. Photographs are loaded from Wikimedia Commons and include attribution and original-source links. The map and external image services need an internet connection.

Street View links open Google's own website. Compare mode juxtaposes independently dated historical photographs rather than Google imagery; historical camera viewpoints may differ. Some older locations are approximate.

No tracking, login or server. Memories and favourites are saved only in your browser. Public map and archive services have their own availability and fair-usage limits.
